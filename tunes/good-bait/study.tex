\begin{study}{Study notes}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
\item Practise the chord voicings with long notes and pauses; understand the voice
  leading between chords; connect close chords. Try ascending or descending voicings.
\item Write down small solos, the bits you know work. Then discard them and see what remains.
\end{itemize}
