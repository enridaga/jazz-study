\begin{notes}[History]
Music by Arthur Schwartz, lyrics by Howard Dietz. Introduced by Jean Sargent in the
Broadway revue \emph{Flying Colors} (Imperial Theatre, opened 15 September 1932, 188 performances).
Leo Reisman and His Orchestra (vocal Frank Luther) took it to the charts in late 1932.
The first jazz recording was by Artie Shaw, in 1939.

\end{notes}
