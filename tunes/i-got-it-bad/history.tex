\begin{notes}[History]
Pop and jazz standard, music by Duke Ellington, lyrics by Paul Francis Webster,
published in 1941. Introduced by Ivie Anderson in the revue \emph{Jump for Joy}
(Mayan Theater, Los Angeles, opened 10 July 1941, 101 performances); she also sang
on the Ellington Orchestra single, Victor 27531. In the Billboard charts of 1941--42:
Duke Ellington (\#13) and Benny Goodman with Peggy Lee (\#25).
Original key G major; form AABA.
