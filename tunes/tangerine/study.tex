\begin{study}{Lesson notes (Session 1, Pete)}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
\item American songbook: learn from one song and look for the same things in other songs.
\item Common forms: AABA, ABAC (Tangerine is ABAC).
\item Lyrics can help: check for the rhymes.
\item Use the harmonic minor when preparing for \ch{ii}{-} and \ch{vi}{-}.
\item The main key is a planet and the others are moons. In Tangerine:
  \ch{I}{} and \ch{IV}{} major, \ch{ii}{-} and \ch{vi}{-} minor. Find the way to know when to play what.
\end{itemize}
\end{study}
