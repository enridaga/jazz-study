\begin{notes}[History]
Music by Richard Rodgers, lyrics by Lorenz Hart, written for Billy Rose's
musical \emph{Jumbo} (Hippodrome, New York, opened 16 November 1935),
where it was introduced by Donald Novis and Gloria Grafton.
Doris Day sang it in the 1962 film \emph{Billy Rose's Jumbo}.

\end{notes}
