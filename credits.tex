\section*{Contributors}
Nerdbook was created and designed by \textbf{Enrico Daga}, who maintains the project.

Nerdbook is written by the Nerdbook contributors: everyone who has contributed a commit to
the \href{https://github.com/enridaga/nerdbook}{GitHub repository} or is mentioned in these
credits.

\section*{Sources}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Lessons.} The tunes, the lead sheets used for the transcriptions, the lesson
  notes and most of the listening selections come from the courses of
  \href{https://www.jazzschool.co.uk/}{Jazzschool}, including its
  \href{https://www.jazzschool.co.uk/repertoire.html}{repertoire lists} and playlists.
  Lesson notes are credited in each tune to the session and the tutor.
\item \textbf{Lead sheets and handouts.} Melodies and changes are transcribed into number
  notation from the lead sheets and handouts used in class; these are not reproduced here.
\item \textbf{Reference works.} Historical notes cite their sources in each tune
  (for example Wikipedia and JazzStandards.com).
\item \textbf{The songs} belong to their composers, lyricists and publishers, credited under
  each title. This book contains study analyses of them.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Use of AI}
This book was made with the help of an AI assistant, Claude, by
\href{https://www.anthropic.com/}{Anthropic}. Claude transcribed lead sheets and handouts
into number notation, drafted study notes, patterns, exercises and background sections,
searched for and cited sources, and wrote the macros and the build tools. Some content,
such as general harmonic explanations, patterns and historical notes, draws on the
model's general knowledge rather than on a cited source.

The contributors directed and reviewed the work, but transcriptions and notes may still contain
errors: check them against the recordings and the original lead sheets. Corrections are
welcome on \href{https://github.com/enridaga/nerdbook}{GitHub}.
