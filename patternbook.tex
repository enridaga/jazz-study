\markboth{How to use the patterns}{How to use the patterns}
Patterns are grouped by harmonic context, that is by what you play them over
(for example a major ii--V--I); within a group they are in alphabetical order.
An alphabetical index of all the patterns is at the end of the book.
Numbers are scale degrees of the key of the target chord. Tabs are in a
reference key (G unless stated), in one position; transpose by moving the shape.
