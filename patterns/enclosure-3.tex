\begin{patternbox}{Enclosure of the 3rd}{\ch{I}{}}
Approach the 3rd of the target chord from a step above and a half step below:
\mel{4\;\sharp2\;\tg{3}}. Works on any target: on \ch{vi}{-7}, enclose its 3rd \mel{\hi{1}}: \mel{\hi{2}\;7\;\tg{\hi{1}}}.
\patterntab{4 #2 3}
\patterntab{2' 7 1'}
\patternwhereline
\end{patternbox}
