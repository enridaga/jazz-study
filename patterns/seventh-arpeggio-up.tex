\begin{patternbox}{Seventh-chord arpeggio 1-3-5-7, up}{any seventh chord}
The four notes of the chord, up from the root, and stop: \mel{1\;3\;5\;7}.
The degrees change with the quality of the chord:
\ch{I}{\Delta7} \mel{1\;3\;5\;7}, \ch{I}{7} \mel{1\;3\;5\;\flat7},
\ch{i}{-7} \mel{1\;\flat3\;5\;\flat7}, \ch{i}{\text{\o}7} \mel{1\;\flat3\;\flat5\;\flat7}.
A simpler version of \emph{Arpeggio 3--9 up, down through V}.
\patterntab{1 3 5 7}
\patternwhereline
\end{patternbox}
