it slips back down to \ch{I}{}. \ch{\flat VI}{7} can also be read as the tritone
substitute of \ch{II}{7} (V/V). Line: \mel{\flat6\;\flat6\;5\;4\;\flat6\;5\;\sharp\lo{4}} $|$ \mel{\tg{\lo{5}}}.
