\begin{patternbox}{Same riff, different chords}{\ch{I}{} $|$ \ch{ii}{-7} $|$ \ch{V}{7}}
Keep the riff \mel{\lo{5}\;1\;3\;5\;5\;4} and let the harmony change under it:
the same notes take a new function on each chord.
On \ch{I}{}: 5, 1, 3; on \ch{ii}{-7}: 11, $\flat$7, 9; on \ch{V}{7}: 1, 11 (sus), 13.

\patternwhereline
\end{patternbox}
