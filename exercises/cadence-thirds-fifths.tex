\begin{study}{Exercise \theexercise: on a cadence, join the thirds or the fifths}
On each cadence of the tune, move from the 3rd of one chord to the 3rd of the next
(or from 5th to 5th), by step where possible. Do it in all keys:

\smallskip
\noindent
$\square$~C \quad $\square$~F \quad $\square$~B$\flat$ \quad $\square$~E$\flat$ \quad
$\square$~A$\flat$ \quad $\square$~D$\flat$ \quad $\square$~G$\flat$ \quad
$\square$~B \quad $\square$~E \quad $\square$~A \quad $\square$~D \quad $\square$~G
\end{study}
